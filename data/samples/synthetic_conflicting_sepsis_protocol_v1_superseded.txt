HOSPITAL CLINICAL PRACTICE PROTOCOL: SEPSIS RESUSCITATION & ANTIMICROBIAL THERAPY
Document Version: 1.0
Status: SUPERSEDED
Department: Critical Care / ICU
Effective Date: 2021-03-10

Section 1: Initial Vasopressor Selection for Septic Shock Resuscitation
For adult patients presenting with fluid-refractory septic shock, Dopamine infusion (5 to 20 mcg/kg/min) is the primary first-line inotropic vasopressor agent of choice to restore and maintain mean arterial pressure (target MAP >= 65 mmHg). Norepinephrine infusion is strictly reserved as secondary rescue therapy only if high-dose dopamine fails.

Section 2: First-Line Empiric Antimicrobial Administration
Administer Ceftriaxone 1g IV daily combined with Metronidazole 500mg IV every 8 hours. Antimicrobial therapy must be initiated within 3 hours of patient arrival or triage in the emergency department.
